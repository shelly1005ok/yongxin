04 / FAQ

Before you send an RFQ.

These answers help you define the ASTM A270 requirement that needs to be quoted.

Can I specify 304L or 316L?

Yes. State the required grade with your dimensional and application requirement.

What should I include in an ASTM A270 enquiry?

Include standard, grade, OD, wall thickness, length, quantity, application and any surface, inspection, document or drawing requirements.

Which dimensions are available?

The availability table is a starting point. The final product specification is confirmed against your RFQ and order requirements.

Can I request documentation?

State the documentation you require, such as MTR or inspection records, when sending the RFQ so the team can confirm scope and availability.
