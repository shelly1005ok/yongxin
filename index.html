04 / FAQ

Before you send an RFQ.

These answers help you define the ASTM A270 requirement that needs to be quoted.

Do you supply ASTM A270 sanitary tube meeting 3-A requirements?

Yes. This Canada-focused page is for ASTM A270 sanitary tube meeting 3-A requirements. Send the required OD, wall thickness, length, grade and application for a technical discussion.

Can I specify 304L or 316L?

Yes. 304L and 316L are available for this ASTM A270 sanitary tube programme. Please state the required grade with your dimensional requirement.

Which ASTM A270 sizes and wall thicknesses are available?

Table A shows the catalogue wall-thickness availability matrix. Table B separately shows the 3-A standard wall and OD tolerances.

What should I include in an ASTM A270 enquiry?

Include the standard, grade, OD, wall thickness, length, quantity, application and any surface, inspection, document or drawing requirements.

What is the normal production cycle and enquiry response time?

Typical production is about 30–45 days. For special project timing, state your requirement in the enquiry. We aim to reply within one business day.
